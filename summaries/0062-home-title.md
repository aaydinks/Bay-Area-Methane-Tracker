# PR #61: A short title on the home page

**Status:** open · [pull request](https://github.com/aaydinks/Bay-Area-Methane-Tracker/pull/61)

## In one sentence

The home page now opens with the name "Bay Area Methane Watch" in dark green and one line saying
what the site is, instead of a long question and two sentences.

## Why it was needed

The first words a visitor saw were a long question ("How persistently do Bay Area landfills leak
methane, and did repairs change that?") followed by more explanation. The owner asked for the name
alone, at the same size and weight, with a plain one-line description under it.

## What it adds

- The title "Bay Area Methane Watch", bold and at the same size as before, in the site's darker
  green.
- The line under it: "A catalog of past methane plumes and analyses for Bay Area landfills."
- The header keeps the retrospective framing and "screening indication" wording on every page,
  so the required wording is still there.
