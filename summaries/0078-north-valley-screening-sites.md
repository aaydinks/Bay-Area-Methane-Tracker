# PR #78: The North Valley landfills join the site list

**Status:** merged 2026-11-21 · [pull request](https://github.com/aaydinks/Bay-Area-Methane-Tracker/pull/78)

## In one sentence

The site list grows from 33 to 52 landfills by adding 19 permitted landfills in Merced, Sacramento,
San Joaquin, Stanislaus and Yolo counties, all in the lightly watched "screening" tier.

## Why it was needed

The previous step switched on the North Valley as a region and proposed its landfills for a hand
check. The owner checked the list on 2026-11-21 and approved 19 of the 20. This step adds them, so
the review copy can show where satellites and aircraft have already seen methane there.

## What it adds

- **19 landfills**, chosen from CalRecycle's list by the same rule as the Bay Area ones: permitted
  landfills, open or closed.
- **An outline for each.** Eleven use the outline CalRecycle's staff drew from permit maps (as of
  September 2021). Eight closed sites have a provisional circle of 750 m around CalRecycle's
  reference point.
- **Short names**, such as "Kiefer Landfill", with the name CalRecycle uses kept beside each.
- **A record of the one left out.** The Aerojet Waste Consolidation Unit in Sacramento County is
  listed by CalRecycle as planned. The owner left it out, and the reason is written down so the
  choosing tool does not propose it again.

Nothing on the public website changes. The new sites appear only on the locked review copy, with
satellite passes and plume dots and no estimates.

## What we learned

- **Eight of the new sites have plumes on record**, counted on the outline or within 500 m of it:
  Yolo County Central (52), Kiefer (36), Forward (25), Highway 59 (4), Foothill (3), North County
  (3), Fink Road (1) and UC Davis (1). These counts include plumes Carbon Mapper tags with a sector
  other than waste, and NASA's plume outlines; a later step decides how to treat the first kind.
- **Three air districts are involved**, read from the state's 2004 district map: San Joaquin Valley
  (11 sites), Sacramento Metro (6) and Yolo-Solano (2). All readings are unconfirmed.
- **The earlier sites are untouched.** A trial of the twice-weekly run with 52 sites gave the same
  passes, estimates and details for the 33 earlier sites, and carried the official model fit.
- **132 plumes gained a tie to a new site.** None was tied to any site before. On the public map
  they stay plain dots, or stay hidden, exactly as before.
- **A gap to close before the next public update.** A plume tied to no site carries its distance
  to the nearest landfill on the list, including landfills still under review. The website does not
  show that number, but a public copy would carry it. It needs a small fix of its own.
